## Where to go next

- **Official REAPER resources.** The REAPER User Guide (the "ReaperMania"
  community videos and Kenny Gioia's official tutorial series on YouTube) are the
  fastest way to get fluent in the DAW.
- **Get the free tools.** Download the **NAM** plugin from its official site and
  grab free captures and IRs from **TONE3000** — then experiment with the "Try
  this" boxes in Chapters 7–8.
- **Train your ears.** Ear-training tools (e.g., frequency-identification games)
  turn the EQ map of Chapter 9 into instinct faster than reading ever will.
- **Learn loudness.** Read up on **LUFS** and streaming loudness normalization
  (Chapter 3) before you master anything for release.
- **Study reference mixes.** Pull professionally-mixed songs in your genre into
  your DAW and A/B your work against them — the single most valuable habit in
  mixing.

> **A closing note.** No book substitutes for hours at the faders. Use this one
> as a map: when you hit a term or a problem, come back, get the mental model,
> then go make decisions with your ears. That loop — understand, try, listen,
> adjust — is the whole craft.
